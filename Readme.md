# Day 09 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Business Intelligence

## Apex Lark – Data Analytics Internship

### Project Overview
This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Superstore retail sales dataset using Python. The objective is to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, statistical patterns, and correlations to generate meaningful business insights.

### Objectives
- Understand and explore the retail sales dataset.
- Identify top-performing products, categories, regions, and customers.
- Perform descriptive statistical analysis.
- Analyze relationships between numerical variables.
- Create professional business visualizations.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

### Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook / VS Code

### Tasks Performed

**1. Data Understanding**
- Loaded the dataset using Pandas.
- Displayed first and last 10 records.
- Checked dataset information, data types, and memory usage.
- Generated descriptive statistics.
- Identified total rows and columns.

**2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
- Identified products generating the highest profit.
- Analyzed sales by product category.
- Identified the highest revenue-generating region.
- Analyzed city-wise order counts.
- Identified the most profitable customer segment.
- Analyzed monthly sales trends.
- Calculated Average Order Value.
- Identified the Top 10 customers by total sales.

**3. Statistical Analysis**
- Calculated Mean, Median, and Mode.
- Calculated Standard Deviation and Variance.
- Analyzed Quartiles and Interquartile Range (IQR).
- Identified potential outliers using the IQR method.

**4. Correlation Analysis**
- Generated a correlation matrix.
- Created a correlation heatmap.
- Identified the strongest positive and negative correlations.
- Analyzed relationships between sales, profit, discounts, and profit margins.

**5. Data Visualization**
Created the following visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn:
- Monthly Sales Trend – Line Chart
- Sales by Region – Bar Chart
- Profit by Category – Bar Chart
- Sales Distribution – Histogram
- Profit Distribution – Box Plot
- Correlation Heatmap
- Top 10 Customers by Sales – Horizontal Bar Chart
- Product Category Sales – Pie Chart

**6. Business Insights & Recommendations**
Prepared a business insights report covering:
- Executive Summary and Dataset Overview.
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
- Customer Behavior Analysis.
- Product Performance Analysis.
- Regional Performance Analysis.
- Correlation Analysis.
- Five Data-Driven Business Recommendations.

### Key Business Findings

- **Highest Sales Category:** Technology
- **Highest Sales Month:** November 2017
- **Highest Revenue Region:** West
- **Maximum Orders City:** New York City
- **Most Profitable Customer Segment:** Consumer

### Key Correlation Findings

- Sales and Net Sales: 1.00
- Sales and Total Sales: 0.93
- Unit Price and Total Sales: 0.90
- Discount and Profit Margin: -0.86
- Sales and Profit: 0.48

The correlation analysis highlighted the importance of managing discount strategies to protect profit margins.

### Project Deliverables
- Python Script (.py)
- Business Insights Report (.pdf)
- README.md

### Conclusion
This project provided practical experience in performing Exploratory Data Analysis, statistical analysis, correlation analysis, and data visualization using Python. The findings helped identify important business patterns and generate actionable recommendations to support data-driven decision-making.
